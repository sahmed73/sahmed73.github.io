I’m Shihab Ahmed, a PhD researcher in computational tribology at the University of California, Merced.

My research uses molecular simulation and artificial intelligence to understand antioxidant chemistry, reaction mechanisms, and the degradation of lubricants and energy-relevant materials. I’m interested in the space where fundamental molecular insight becomes a better engineering material.

### Research toolkit

- Reactive molecular dynamics with ReaxFF
- Machine-learned interatomic potentials
- Scientific machine learning and generative modeling
- High-performance computing and reproducible workflows

### Outside the lab

I enjoy building computational tools, learning across disciplines, and turning complex technical questions into clear visual and analytical stories.
