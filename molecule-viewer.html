Molecule viewer

Explore a molecule or simulation structure in 3D. Choose a local file, enter a SMILES string, or open an example below.

Supports LAMMPS .data (full, molecular, atomic, or charge atom style), MOL/SDF, PDB, and XYZ. Local files stay in your browser.
Requests a 3D structure from PubChem, with an RDKit fallback. The free RDKit service may take about a minute to wake.
Examples ZDDP: zinc bis(O,O-dibutyl dithiophosphate), with illustrative Zn–S geometry. ADPA: 4,4′-dioctyldiphenylamine.
Appearance

Element colors

Presets set C, H, O, and N colors. You can still adjust each element or atom below.
Load a molecule to choose colors.

Selected atom: none

Click an atom in the viewer to give it its own color.
Element labels
Turn on “Show element labels” beside the viewer. These settings also style the selected atom label.
Simulation box

Load a molecule to begin.

Large structures show up to 120 representative labels. Click any atom to identify it.
SVG and PDF embed the shaded PNG; atoms and bonds are not individually editable. Your browser may limit large image sizes.
Rotation animation Rotation sweeps right to left across the preview from your current view. Drag the molecule to set its starting orientation. Oscillate reaches “To” halfway through the duration, then returns to “From”. Preview restores your view afterward. Video saves as MP4 when supported, otherwise WebM.

Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right drag to pan. LAMMPS files need a Masses section or element comments for element colors. Orthogonal simulation boxes are supported.

I built this molecular viewer with help from Codex (GPT-6-Sol). It is currently in beta.
